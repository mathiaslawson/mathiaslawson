## Mathias Prince Lawson

I work on neurosymbolic AI — combining neural models with explicit symbolic
structure so that a system's reasoning can be inspected rather than trusted on
faith. I'm particularly interested in systems that recognise the limits of their
own competence and defer, which matters most in clinical and public health
settings where an unflagged error is expensive.

**Currently**

- Building retrieval systems grounded in structured knowledge, and extending one
  from flat vector retrieval toward ontology-linked knowledge graphs, so that
  retrieved evidence carries explicit relations rather than similarity scores
  alone.
- Reading and experimenting around modularity and capability isolation in neural
  networks — whether capabilities can be represented as dependent structures
  rather than independent domains.

**Public work**

- [Stable Diffusion concept library](https://huggingface.co/mathiaslawson) —
  released from a DreamBooth fine-tuning run for my undergraduate thesis on
  text-to-image generation of web icons. 210+ downloads.

Most of my recent engineering is closed-source, built under contract for
government and intergovernmental clients in health and public policy. Happy to discuss the architecture and design decisions on
request.
